KURTI POST MAKER
================
Free. No second number. No login to WhatsApp. No ban risk.
You share the vendor's catalogue in, check it, and send it to your buyer groups
yourself through WhatsApp.


PART 1: PUT IT ON YOUR PHONE (one time, about 10 minutes, free)
---------------------------------------------------------------
The phone needs the app at a web address so it can appear in WhatsApp's Share menu.
GitHub Pages hosts it free. It is separate from pickupdvmtechsolves.com and does
not touch it.

 1. On your laptop, go to github.com and sign up (free).
 2. Click "+" (top right) > New repository.
    Name: kurti-posts     Choose: Public     Click "Create repository".
 3. Click "uploading an existing file". Drag in ALL the files from this folder
    (index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, jszip.min.js, icon-192.png, icon-512.png).
    Click "Commit changes".
 4. Click Settings (in the repository) > Pages.
    Source: Deploy from a branch. Branch: main, folder: / (root). Click Save.
 5. Wait 1 to 2 minutes and refresh. It shows your address, like:
       https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/kurti-posts/
 6. On your Android phone, open that address in Chrome.
    Tap the three dots > Add to Home screen > Install.

"Kurti Posts" is now an app on your phone and appears in WhatsApp's Share list.
Your designs, settings and codes stay on your phone, not on GitHub.


PART 2: DAILY USE ON THE PHONE
------------------------------
Bring a catalogue in (pick one):
  - Whole vendor chat: open it > three dots > More > Export chat > Include media >
    Kurti Posts. Every catalogue in the chat shows as a card. Tap one.
  - One catalogue: long-press the vendor's photos > Share > Kurti Posts.
    Then copy their text in WhatsApp and tap "Paste copied text".

Then:
  1. Check the details found. Change anything that is wrong.
  2. Set your rate. Tap photos to leave any out.
  3. Tick "Rate approved" and "Photos approved".
  4. Tap "Share to WhatsApp" > WhatsApp > tick your buyer groups (up to 5 at a
     time) > Send. Photos and the post go together.
  5. Tap "Next design".


ON THE LAPTOP (optional)
------------------------
Double-click index.html. Drag the exported zip, photos or the vendor's text onto
the page. Add your groups in Settings to get a "Send to <group>" button for each.


CHANGE THINGS
-------------
Settings at the bottom of the app: margin, code prefix, post format, keywords.
