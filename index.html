Kurti Post Maker

Paste a supplier's WhatsApp message, approve your rate and photos, and copy a buyer-ready post in the same format every time. Runs entirely on this device. No AI, no API, no billing.

  1. 1 Import a WhatsApp chat or paste a message
  2. 2 Check the details it found
  3. 3 Approve rate and photos
  4. 4 Copy post, send to buyer group

Import from WhatsApp text, photos and videos together

  1. Whole vendor chat, on your phone: open it in WhatsApp, tap ⋮ > More > Export chat > Include media and pick Kurti Posts. Every catalogue appears below as a card.
  2. One catalogue, on your phone: long-press the vendor's photos, tap Share > Kurti Posts, then copy their text and tap Paste copied text.
  3. On a laptop: drag the exported zip, photos or the vendor's text onto this page.

Every chat you import is remembered. Next time you import the same chat, only new catalogues appear.

Keep phone and laptop the same

Make a sync file on one device and open it on the other. Vendors, catalogues, posted designs, saved photos and settings from both sides are merged, nothing is overwritten. Send it to yourself on WhatsApp, then on the other device open it with Open chat zip or photos (or Share > Kurti Posts on the phone).

Photo folder: not chosen yet. Each design's ticked photos are saved in their own folder inside it, named after the code (AE-1001, AE-1002...).

One group per line: Group name | invite link. To get a group's link in WhatsApp: open the group, click its name, then Invite via link (or Invite to group via link), and copy the link. You must already be in the group.

Fields: {code} {type} {fabric} {work} {sleeve} {length} {sizes} {colours} {pieces} {price} {moq} {dispatch} {gst} {shipping}. A line is hidden when its field is empty. Add ? to keep the line anyway, e.g. {gst?}. Wrap words in *stars* for WhatsApp bold.

Keywords: one line per result, written as Shown in post: word, other word. Higher lines win, so keep specific words above general ones. Hindi and Gujarati words in English letters work too.
